\title{\textbf{Corrections and validation record}\\[4pt]
\large What was not working in the original \texttt{layer\_resolved} scripts, what was changed in
\texttt{g0w\_heterostructure}, and how the old and new results compare}
\author{}
\date{\today}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
These notes document, in a self-contained way, the physics and the numerics implemented in the
\texttt{g0w} package: the layer-resolved Coulomb kernel of $N$ two-dimensional sheets in a stratified,
possibly gated and dispersive dielectric; the random-phase-approximation (RPA) dielectric matrix and
screened interaction of a stack of decoupled electronic subsystems; the one-shot G$_0$W
(correlation) self-energy in the full-frequency and plasmon-pole forms, including its band-matrix
generalisation for multiband samples; the Dyson equation and the electronic spectral function; and the
differential conductance of a quantum twisting microscope (QTM) in the limit of a monolayer-graphene tip
with a small Fermi surface. Two samples are worked out in detail: doped monolayer graphene
(SLG\,$|$\,hBN\,$|$\,SLG) and twisted bilayer graphene (SLG tip\,$|$\,hBN\,$|$\,TBG). Every equation is
followed by the name of the function that implements it, every approximation is listed explicitly, and
the corrections made with respect to the original working scripts are collected in \cref{app:changes}.
\end{abstract}

\begin{center}
\fbox{\parbox{0.92\textwidth}{\small\textbf{About this document.} This is the development record of the package,
kept as a reference. Besides the theory, it compares the package with the original scripts in
\texttt{layer\_resolved/} (\texttt{test\_v2\_7}, \texttt{test\_SLG\_hBN\_SLG}, \texttt{test\_TBG\_hBN\_SLG}, \texttt{test\_TBG}):
\cref{app:changes} lists every correction with its reason and the switch that restores the old behaviour, and
\cref{sec:valleys,sec:qtmtbg,sec:resqtmtbg} show old and new results side by side. The self-contained notes,
without these remarks, are \texttt{docs/notes.pdf}.}}
\end{center}
